Privacy-preserving community reporting

See a problem in your neighbourhood? Put it on the record.

Report broken lighting, unsafe crossings, obstructions and waterlogging with a photo and a pin. SurakshaMap groups nearby reports into hotspots, scores risk transparently, and points to a practical next step.

Track your report with a token, follow status changes, and see the recommended intervention on the dashboard.

Responsible use. This prototype does not predict crime, identify people, or replace human verification. It is a visibility tool for public-space maintenance and safety issues.
